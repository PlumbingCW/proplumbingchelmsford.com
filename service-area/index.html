The Chelmsford villages

Inside Chelmsford we serve the town center, North Chelmsford, South Chelmsford, West Chelmsford and East Chelmsford. The Westlands, the 1920s neighborhood on the Lowell side of town, has its own page as well. North Chelmsford has its own ZIP, 01863, and the rest of the town uses 01824. Chelmsford has three separate water districts, so when you call, mention your street.

Our largest services are drain cleaning, water heater repair and burst pipe repair, and they are available in every village and every town on this page.

Roads shape how we dispatch. Route 3, Interstate 495, Route 110 and Route 4 cross Chelmsford, and a call from the north end of town and a call from the south end are different drives. Tell us the village and the cross street and we plan around it.

What changes from town to town

The plumbing is the same, but the utilities are not. Water, sewer, permits and inspections run through each town's own offices, and some homes use a private well or a septic system instead of town services. That is why the first questions we ask are the street and the town.

Outside these towns

If you live outside the towns listed, call anyway. Dispatch can tell you whether a plumber can reach your address and how long it might take. We would rather tell you honestly that we cannot cover an address than promise a time we cannot keep. For anything involving gas odors, fire or an injury, call 911 first.

Property managers and landlords with buildings in more than one of these towns can use the same number for all of them. Tell dispatch which building and which unit is affected, who can let the plumber in and who approves the work. That keeps a multi-property call from turning into a string of questions at the door.

Seasonal calls across the area

Across all five towns, the calls follow the weather. Cold snaps bring frozen and burst pipes, so know where your main shutoff is before winter. Thaw and heavy rain bring sump pump failures and basement water. Warm months bring drain clogs, water heater failures and outdoor spigot leaks.

Homes on private wells have different winter risks than homes on town water, because a burst pipe keeps pumping until the well pump is shut off. Tell dispatch which system you have, and we adjust the instructions we give you.

Before you call

  • Know your main water shutoff and the breaker for your water heater.
  • Note your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Have your water district or town utility contact handy for billing and meter questions.
  • Photograph any damage for your records and your insurer.
