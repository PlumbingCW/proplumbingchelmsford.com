Clogged drain in Chelmsford? Start here

When water stops draining in Chelmsford, the first job is to stop adding more of it. Our emergency drain cleaning service is open 24/7, so a backed-up kitchen sink at midnight gets the same answer as a slow tub on a Tuesday morning. Until the plumber arrives, stop running the dishwasher and washing machine, and keep everyone away from standing water near outlets.

Drain cleaning covers four different jobs that look alike from the bathroom floor. They are a clog in one fixture trap, a blockage in a branch line, a stoppage in the main line that serves the whole house, and a slow drain that is really a pipe problem. The right tool depends on which one you have, and guessing wrong is how people end up buying three bottles of cleaner and still calling us.

Clearing a drain is also one of the few plumbing jobs the state treats as minor repair work. Massachusetts says a blocked drain does not need a permit, although replacing or relocating pipe does. If we find that clearing the line will not hold, we explain the repair first and what the town expects before we do it.

Is your clogged drain an emergency right now?

Treat it as urgent if water is rising toward the floor, if more than one fixture is gurgling or backing up, or if you smell sewage. Those signs usually point to the main line rather than a single trap. A lone kitchen sink that drains slowly can wait for a same-day visit when one is open, but it should not wait for weeks.

Kitchen sinks, tubs and bathroom sinks: what usually clogs them

Kitchen drains clog with grease and food that cools and coats the pipe wall. Bathroom sinks and tubs clog with hair, soap film and toothpaste that bind into a plug just past the trap. Both are usually branch-line jobs that a hand auger or cable machine clears in one visit.

Kitchen lines tied to a garbage disposal clog a little differently. Ground food travels farther before it settles, so the blockage sits deeper than people expect. If the sink backs up into the second basin, the clog is past the tailpiece, and a plunger will only push it around. A related fix is covered on our garbage disposal repair page.

Main line backups and floor drain overflows

A main line clog stops every drain in the house at once or backs up the lowest fixture first, which in many Chelmsford homes is a basement floor drain or a first-floor tub. Tree roots, a collapsed section and a belly in the line are the usual causes, and a cable machine alone sometimes cannot tell them apart.

Homes tied to the public sewer in Chelmsford connect through a lateral to the town main. The town reports 190 miles of public sewer line and 41 pump stations, and its Sewer Division asks residents to call 978-250-5233 for sewer emergencies. If the stoppage is in the public main rather than your pipe, that call matters.

We can tell you which side of the property line the blockage is on, and we can run a sewer camera inspection to prove it.

Cabling punches a hole through a blockage. Jetting washes the pipe wall. When a clog returns, the honest answer is often a camera, because a line that keeps filling has a reason, and the reason is rarely the last thing that went down the drain.
