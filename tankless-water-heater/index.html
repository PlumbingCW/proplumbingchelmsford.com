Tankless water heaters in Chelmsford: install, repair and flush

Tankless water heater service in Chelmsford covers three jobs: installing a new unit, flushing and descaling one that is losing performance, and repairing one that throws an error code. Plenty of callers tell us the last plumber replaced instead of repaired. We do both, and we tell you which one the unit needs.

A tankless heater heats water as it flows, which saves space and removes the standby loss of a tank. It also asks more from the install. The gas line has to be sized for the burner, the vent has to match the unit, and the water has to be treated if it carries scale.

Tankless failures are rarely sudden. Cold-water sandwiches, error codes and falling flow are early signs. If the unit is leaking or smelling of gas, shut it down and see our emergency water heater repair page.

Flush and descale: why and how often

Scale builds inside the heat exchanger and slows the flow. A flush with a descaling solution and a pump restores it. Annual flushing is typical, and homes with harder water may need it sooner. The town districts publish annual water quality reports. Check yours, and ask us if the numbers point to scale.

Callers often tell us the unit has not been serviced in years. That is fixable. If a flush brings back the flow, you have saved the price of a new unit.

Tankless or tank: how to decide

Tankless suits households that run out of hot water, want to free floor space or plan to stay in the home a long time. A tank suits tight budgets and simple replacements. Compare options on our water heater installation page.

If you are replacing a failed tank with a tankless unit, the gas line and vent usually need work. Ask for that line item up front.

Flushing a tankless heater to keep it efficient

A tankless unit needs a descaling flush on a regular schedule. Mineral scale coats the heat exchanger and reduces flow and efficiency. The flush uses a small pump, hoses and a descaling solution circulated through the unit. Skipping it is the most common reason a tankless heater fails early.
