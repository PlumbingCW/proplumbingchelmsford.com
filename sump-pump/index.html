Sump pump repair and installation in Chelmsford

A sump pump moves groundwater out of a basement pit before it reaches the floor. When it fails, usually during heavy rain or a fast thaw, the basement can flood within hours. Our emergency sump pump service in Chelmsford runs 24/7, and we repair, replace and install pumps and backups.

If your sump is not running and water is rising, check that the pump is plugged in and the breaker is on. Lift the float to see whether the pump starts. If it does not, call. While you wait, move items off the floor and do not enter flooded areas with live electrical equipment.

A pump is a mechanical part, and mechanical parts fail. A backup, battery or water-powered, is what protects you when the main fails or the power goes out.

Why pumps fail

  • Stuck or tangled float switches.
  • Burnt-out motors from age or constant running.
  • Clogged intake or check valve.
  • Power outages with no backup.
  • An undersized pump for the amount of water.

Discharge and where the water goes

Sump discharge must go outside, away from the foundation. It should never be connected to the sanitary sewer line. Check valves keep pumped water from returning to the pit, and frost can freeze outdoor discharge, so slope and termination matter.

Testing your pump before the storm

  1. Check the powerMake sure the pump is plugged into a working outlet, ideally a GFCI.
  2. Pour in waterFill the pit with a bucket until the float rises.
  3. Watch the cycleThe pump should start, empty the pit and shut off cleanly.
  4. Look at the dischargeConfirm water leaves the pipe outside and does not drip back.
  5. Test the backupIf you have a battery backup, switch to it and check it runs.

Do this every few months and before spring thaw. A pump that struggles in a test will fail in a storm.

Warning signs that a pump is near the end

  • It runs without stopping even in dry weather.
  • It makes grinding, rattling or humming noises.
  • The float sticks or fails to rise.
  • Rust or heavy sediment shows in the pit.
  • It is more than a decade old.

Replacing a pump on your schedule beats replacing it at two in the morning. A new pump is usually a short job, and the old one can be checked for cause so the new one lasts longer. See basement waterproofing if the pit fills too often.
