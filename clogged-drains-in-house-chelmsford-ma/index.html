Clogged Drains in Your Chelmsford, MA House

How to tell a simple clog from a deeper problem, what you can safely try and when to call.

Call (855) 575-2890

One slow drain or all of them

The most useful question about a clog is how many drains are affected. One slow sink is a local clog. A tub and a toilet that gurgle together point to a branch line. Every fixture backing up points to the main line. The answer tells you which tool and which kind of call you need.

This guide sorts the cases and explains what to try before you call. When the situation is urgent, see our drain cleaning page. For the main line, see sewer line cleaning.

What you can safely try

  1. Remove standing waterBail out what you can and keep it out of electrical outlets.
  2. Use a plungerSeal the overflow on a sink or tub, then plunge firmly.
  3. Clean the trapPut a bucket under it and unscrew the slip nuts by hand.
  4. Use a hand augerFeed it in until you feel the clog and work it back and forth.
  5. Flush with hot waterRun hot, not boiling, water to rinse residue.

Skip chemical drain cleaners. They can damage pipes, they are dangerous to a plumber who has to open the line later and they often do not clear a serious clog.

Signs of a bigger problem

  • Clogs that return within days.
  • More than one fixture backing up.
  • Sewage smell near drains or outside.
  • Slow drains after a heavy rain.
  • Water rising in a basement floor drain.

These suggest roots, a belly or a collapse. A camera inspection shows what a plunger cannot.

What causes most household clogs

Kitchen drains clog with grease, food and soap. Bathroom drains clog with hair and soap scum. Toilets clog with wipes, paper and anything flushed that should not be. Older pipes with rough walls catch debris faster than smooth ones.

The cheapest fix is prevention. Use drain screens, scrape plates, keep grease out and never flush wipes, even those labeled flushable.

Kitchen, bathroom and laundry clogs differ

RoomUsual causeBetter habit.
KitchenGrease, food, soap filmWipe pans, use a strainer.

Shower or tubHair and soap scumFit a mesh screen.
LaundryLint and detergent residueUse a lint trap on the hose.
ToiletWipes and too much paperFlush only paper and waste.

Each room clogs for its own reasons, so the fix and the prevention differ. A single habit change in the kitchen often prevents most repeat calls.

Why a vent can make drains slow

Every drain needs air behind it. The vent stack runs up through the roof and lets air in as water leaves, so the water flows smoothly. A blocked vent, from a leaf pile, a bird nest or a frost cap in winter, makes drains gurgle and drain slowly even when the pipes are clear.

If several drains in a house gurgle at once and a plunger or cable does not help, mention it when you call. A vent check is a quick step, and clearing it is a different fix from cleaning a line.

How we clear a branch line or a main

  1. Find the access pointA cleanout, a removed trap or a toilet pull gives us a way in.
  2. Choose the toolA hand auger for a short run, a drum machine for a longer branch, a jet for heavy buildup.
  3. Clear and flushWe cut through the clog, pull what we can and flush with water.
  4. Check the flowWe run several fixtures at once to confirm the line is open.
  5. Camera if it returnsA repeat clog is worth a look inside the pipe.

A good cleaning ends with a flow test, not just a quiet drain.

Cost drivers for drain cleaning

Price depends on how deep the clog sits, how easy the access is, which tool is needed and what time of day you call. A fixture clog is the simplest, a branch line costs more and a main line with a camera costs most. We give a range on the phone and confirm once we see the line.

An emergency call at night costs more than a scheduled visit, so a slow drain deserves attention before it turns into a backup.
