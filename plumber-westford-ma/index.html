Wells, PFAS and what it means for your plumbing

Westford has both town water and private wells. The town's Water Department has said its Country Road 2 well was out of service from August 2021 because of elevated PFAS, and was later brought back for limited summer operation under a MassDEP-approved plan. The department reports it is pursuing longer-term treatment. The state's current limit is 20 parts per trillion for the sum of six PFAS compounds.

What does that mean for a plumber? Treatment equipment, filters and softeners add fixtures to a home, and each needs correct plumbing and a bypass. If you are adding treatment, our plumbing installation team handles the hookups. For private wells, the Westford Health Department runs a PFAS sampling program, so call them about testing.
