Privacy policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you contact Chelmsford Pro Plumbing. We take calls and do not operate web forms.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

What we collect

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, service address and a description of the problem. We use it to dispatch and complete your service.

How we use it
