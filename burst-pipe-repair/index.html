Burst pipe in Chelmsford? Do these three things first

If a pipe has burst, find the main shutoff and close it before you do anything else. Then open the lowest faucet to drain the line, switch off power to wet areas if you can reach the panel safely, and call our 24/7 emergency line. Everything after that is repair work, and it can wait a few minutes. The water cannot.

Burst pipe repair covers more than a split copper tube. It includes a PEX line that popped off a fitting, a galvanized section that corroded through, a supply valve behind a washing machine that let go, and a frozen line that cracked and only showed itself when the thaw started. Each one needs a different repair, and each one has a different clean-up risk.

We handle the plumbing side, which means stopping the leak, replacing the damaged section and testing the line. Water damage clean-up and the insurance claim are separate steps, and we explain what to document for both.

When a burst pipe is an emergency and when it is not

Active flow you cannot stop is an emergency. So is water coming through a ceiling, water near the electrical panel, or any leak that appears after a hard freeze. A slow weep at a compression fitting under a sink is urgent but usually manageable with the supply valve closed until a same-day visit.

Frozen pipes, thaw bursts and New England winters

Pipes that freeze rarely burst where the ice sits. They burst where pressure builds between the ice plug and a closed faucet, and the split often shows only after the ice melts. That is why the real flood sometimes starts the afternoon the weather warms up.

The usual trouble spots are pipes in exterior walls, runs over an unheated garage, hose bibs that were not drained, and lines in crawl spaces. If a faucet runs a trickle or stops during a cold snap, keep the cabinet doors open, add gentle heat, and call before the pipe has a chance to split. Ahead of a freeze, repiping a run that keeps freezing can cost less than another repair.

Repair options for a burst pipe

Pipe materialTypical repairNotes.
CopperCut out the section, sweat in new copper or use press fittingsCommon in older homes, can pinhole before it splits.
PEXReplace the damaged run, crimp or expansion fittingsHandles freezing better than rigid pipe but can still fail at fittings.
Galvanized steelReplace the section, consider the whole runCorrodes from the inside, so one failure often means more are coming.
PVC or CPVCCut and re-glue with the right primer and cementCold makes plastic brittle.

A clean, honest repair means replacing the failed section back to sound pipe. A patch clamp may stop a flood for a night, but it is not a permanent repair, and we say which one we are doing.

Where the water went: checking behind walls and under floors

A burst pipe often leaves water in places you cannot see, inside wall cavities and under flooring. After the repair, we check the line under pressure and look for secondary leaks. If something still seems off, a visit from our leak detection team can trace hidden moisture before it turns into mold.

If the line that burst fed a water heater, we will also check the unit. A sudden pressure surge or drained tank can trip safety devices. See our water heater repair page if the hot water is down after the repair.
