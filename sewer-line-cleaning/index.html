Sewer line cleaning in Chelmsford, from first gurgle to flow restored

Sewer line cleaning clears the pipe between your house and the sewer main. When that line is blocked, every drain in the house is affected, so it is one of the most disruptive plumbing failures a home can have. Our emergency sewer cleaning service in Chelmsford runs 24/7 and starts with a plan: locate the cleanout, run the right tool and show you what came out.

Early signs are easy to miss. A toilet that gurgles when the washer drains, a basement floor drain that bubbles, a tub that slowly rises with the shower. If you notice any of them, stop using water and call. Waiting often turns a gurgle into a backup.

Cleaning is not repair. If the line has a defect, we tell you after the camera and explain repair options before anything else.

Backup in progress: what to do while you wait

A main line backup rarely takes only one fixture. If the lowest drain in the house is overflowing, protect floors and electronics and stay out of the water.

No waterHold laundry, dishwasher and showers.
Protect belongingsLift items off the floor and move rugs.

What clogs a sewer line

  • Grease and food that cooled and hardened inside the pipe.
  • Roots from nearby trees entering joints.
  • Wipes and paper products that do not break down.
  • A sag in the line that collects solids.
  • Mineral scale in older metal pipes.

Cable or jetting: choosing the tool

A cable machine cuts through roots and opens a path fast. Jetting washes the wall and clears buildup. A common sequence is cable first for access, then jetting to restore the pipe's full diameter. See hydro jetting for when we use it.

A camera follow-up confirms the result and records the condition of the line. If the line keeps clogging, the camera is how we find out why.

Cleaning a main line step by step

  1. Find the cleanoutWe locate the access point, usually in the basement, crawl space or outside near the foundation.
  2. Open and relieveWe open the cap slowly to relieve any backed-up water.
  3. Cable through the blockageA drum machine cuts a path through the clog.
  4. Pull debris and flushWe retrieve what we can and flush the line with water.
  5. Check flowWe run water and confirm the fixtures drain freely.

If the line backs up again soon, the cause is likely deeper than a single clog. A camera inspection shows the reason.

After the line is clear

A clean line does not mean the problem is solved for good. Ask what we found on the cable, because roots, grease and scale each point to a different next step. Grease calls for a change in kitchen habits and sometimes a jetting pass. Roots call for a camera look and probably a repair at the joint.

A line that was blocked by an object, such as a toy or a wad of wipes, may be fine once it is out.

We tell you what came out and what we suspect, so you can decide whether to book a follow-up.
