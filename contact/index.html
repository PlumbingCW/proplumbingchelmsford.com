Where we serve

Our call line covers Chelmsford, Westford, Billerica, Lowell and Tyngsborough. This site does not have a walk-in office, so call or email rather than visiting.

What to tell us when you call

  • Your street address and village or town.
  • What is happening, and whether the water or fuel is shut off.
  • The age of the house and the fuel type for heating calls.
  • Whether anyone is hurt or whether there is a gas smell. If so, leave and call 911 first.
