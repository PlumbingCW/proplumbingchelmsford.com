Commercial plumbing in Chelmsford, including after-hours calls

Commercial plumbing problems cost money by the hour. A backed-up floor drain in a shop, a leaking supply in a retail space or a failed restroom fixture can force a closure. Our emergency commercial plumbing line in Chelmsford runs 24/7, and we take calls from owners, tenants and property managers alike.

When you call, tell us the building type, where the problem is, and whether you can shut water off. If water is moving, close the nearest shutoff. If a floor drain is overflowing, keep the area clear and let us know what the building handles, such as food service or light industrial use.

Commercial work is often scoped differently from residential, and we say plainly what we can and cannot take on. Large mechanical or fire-protection projects belong to specialty contractors. Day-to-day plumbing repair, drain clearing, fixture work and leak response are ours.

After-hours emergencies at a business

Plumbing failures at a business tend to happen when no one is there, which means water runs longer before anyone notices. If you receive an alarm call, shut the main if you can do so safely and call us.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Close the valve serving the affected fixture or the main.
  • Block off wet floors with signs or cones so customers and staff do not slip.
  • Keep staff out of any area where sewage has backed up.
  • Move stock and equipment off the floor if water is spreading.
  • Tell dispatch whether the space is open to the public.

What we handle for commercial properties

  • Clogged restroom and floor drains, including drain cleaning and jetting.
  • Toilet, sink and faucet repairs and replacements.
  • Water heater repair and replacement in smaller commercial settings.
  • Leak detection and burst pipe repair.
  • Sewer backups, camera inspection and cleaning.
  • Routine drain maintenance for kitchens and shops.

Landlords, tenants and property managers

Work orders from property managers often arrive with a tenant contact and a reference number. We can work with that. When a repair involves who pays, tenant or landlord, we document what we found so the decision is clear.

A building with several tenants or a shared lateral raises the stakes for a backup. A camera look at the shared line (sewer inspection) helps identify where it sits.

Planning around business hours

A restroom that is out of service costs a business more than the repair does. We schedule commercial work for before opening, after close or on a weekend whenever the job allows. For an emergency we send a plumber at once and work to get the fixtures back in service, then return for the permanent fix if the first visit was a stopgap.

Tell us what kind of building it is, who manages it and who can authorize work. A tenant, a landlord and a property manager can all be responsible for different parts, and knowing who to bill avoids delay.
