Lowell, neighborhood by neighborhood

Lowell is a city of about 115,500 people, the fifth largest in Massachusetts, with eight neighborhoods including Belvidere, Pawtucketville, the Highlands, Centralville and the Acre. It borders Chelmsford directly, and the same Route 3 and Route 110 roads that serve Chelmsford lead there. Emergency dispatch for Lowell runs around the clock.

Lowell is a separate market with many local plumbers, and we do not pretend otherwise. What we offer is a 24/7 line for the area and a clear price before work starts.

Older buildings, canal-era streets

Lowell grew with the mills. The National Park Service preserves about 5.6 miles of largely restored canals and many historic mill sites. In and around that older core, the plumbing is often as old as the buildings. Aging cast iron, galvanized steel and long-buried lines are likely suspects when leaks and backups repeat.

If you have repeated pinhole leaks, our repiping page explains the options. For recurring clogs, ask for a sewer camera inspection. For businesses in older commercial buildings, see commercial plumbing.

Water, lead service lines and your meter

The Lowell Regional Water Utility serves Lowell, Dracut, Tyngsboro and Chelmsford, with about 215 miles of water main and roughly 26,000 service connections. The utility is running a lead service line inventory to identify service line materials and says its corrosion control program keeps lead concentration well below the state limit.

For homeowners, the practical step is to learn what your service line is made of and ask the utility. If your meter runs with everything off, see leak detection.

Multifamily and commercial buildings

A plumbing problem in a shared building is a coordination problem as well. One unit's leak is another unit's ceiling stain, and a shared drain stack backs up into the lowest unit. We ask who manages the building, who can authorize the work and which units are affected. That lets us shut off water to the right section without leaving everyone dry.

Landlords and property managers can call the 24/7 line and set up a scheduled visit for non-urgent work.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Lowell?

Yes. Lowell has four ZIP codes, 01850, 01851, 01852 and 01854, covering the Acre and downtown, the Highlands and Belvidere, Centralville, and Pawtucketville. We dispatch to all four. Nearby Dracut, just north of Lowell, is part of the same coverage area.

Permits and inspections in Lowell run through the city, and the work itself is held to the state plumbing code, 248 CMR, which is the same standard statewide.
