Faucet and sink repair in Chelmsford

Faucet and sink repair in Chelmsford covers dripping spouts, leaking handles, cracked supply lines, stuck shutoff valves and sink installs. The emergency version is the valve that will not shut off or the line that has burst under a cabinet. We answer the 24/7 line for those, and we schedule routine drips for the next available visit.

If a fixture is flooding, close the shutoff valve under the sink. If that valve is stuck or leaking, close the main. Do not force a corroded valve, because the stem can break.

Many faucet calls turn out to be cartridge or washer jobs. Others point to older fixtures worth replacing. We explain the difference and price both.

A valve that will not shut off

A shutoff valve that has seized or leaks around the stem leaves you unable to isolate a fixture. In an older house, valves can sit untouched for decades and fail when finally used. Replacing them while the system is calm is cheaper than doing it in a flood.

A faucet that is more than a decade old may use parts that are hard to find. At that point, a new faucet costs little more than parts and labor.

Under-sink leaks and supply lines

Water under a sink might come from the supply lines, the drain trap or the faucet itself. Dry the cabinet, run the water and watch. A slow drip at a compression nut usually just needs tightening. A braided line with a rusty crimp needs replacing.

If the leak keeps returning, a hidden source may be involved. See leak detection. For kitchen sinks tied to a disposal, garbage disposal repair may apply.

Knowing the type lets you decide between a repair kit and a new faucet. A drip from a cartridge faucet is usually a ten minute fix. A pitted seat or a corroded body is a reason to replace. See plumbing installation if you want a new fixture fitted instead.
